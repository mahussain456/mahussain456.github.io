Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This is the personal portfolio of Ahmed Hussain at mahussain456.github.io. It's a simple website that shows my work. This page explains exactly what happens to your data when you visit.

The short version

  • This site sets no cookies.
  • It uses no analytics or tracking tools.
  • It has no forms and no accounts. I don't collect your name, email or anything else through the site.
  • I don't sell or share personal data, because the site doesn't collect any.

Services that see your visit

Like any website, loading this page means your browser connects to a few servers. Each one can see standard technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request.

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security and to keep the service running. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site's fonts. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • Hacker News Search by Algolia supplies the "AI, today" news section. Your browser fetches the headlines directly from it. See the Algolia Privacy Policy.

I don't receive or store any of this information myself.

Links to other websites

This site links to other sites, including GlobalSync AI, news articles, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram and YouTube. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and I'm not responsible for how they handle your data.

If you email me

If you contact me at m.ahmedhussain9@gmail.com, I'll use your email address and message only to reply to you. I won't add you to any mailing list. You can ask me to delete our conversation at any time.

Children

This site isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the site changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding analytics, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
